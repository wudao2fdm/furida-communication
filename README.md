# Furida

An HTML-based home billing management system — fully local, no internet required, private and secure, easy to use, with one-click Excel export.

# Website

Official website: [https://furida-one.195868.xyz](https://furida-one.195868.xyz)

## Features

- **Fully Local & Private**: Your billing data stays entirely on your own computer and never leaves it
- **Works Offline**: Runs completely offline, no data is ever uploaded
- **No Installation**: No extra software needed — just open and go
- **Smart Analytics**: Visualize your family income and expenses with beautiful charts
- **Excel Export**: Export your bills to Excel with one click for backup and analysis

## Download

Download the latest `furida.html` from the [Releases](https://github.com/wudao2fdm/furida-communication/releases/latest) page and open it in your browser (Chrome, Edge, etc.).
